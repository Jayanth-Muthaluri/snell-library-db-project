## 1. Problem Statement

Thousands of students, faculty, and staff use Snell Library every week to borrow books and media, book study rooms, and check out equipment like laptops and cameras. Right now, all of that activity is tracked through separate, disconnected records. There's no single place that shows what's checked out, what's overdue, which rooms are free, or who still owes a fine. That makes it slow for staff to answer even simple questions, and it means members can't check real time availability before showing up. Library management also has no real way to see how the collection or the spaces are actually being used over time. What's needed is one central database, paired with a simple dashboard, that keeps all of this information consistent, enforces the lending and reservation rules automatically, and gives staff a quick view of what's happening in the library at any moment.

## 2. Mission Statement

The Snell Library database exists to store and manage the information behind the library's day to day services. That includes cataloging and lending physical and digital items, handling reservations for study spaces and loanable technology, and keeping track of member activity and fines. On top of that, the data gets surfaced through a dashboard so staff can help borrowers quickly and keep an eye on how the library is running.

## 3. Mission Objectives

- Keep track of members (students, faculty, and staff) and their borrowing status
- Keep track of catalog items (books, journals, media) and their individual copies
- Keep track of loans, including check outs, due dates, returns, and renewals
- Keep track of holds and requests for items that are currently checked out
- Keep track of study rooms and loanable technology such as laptops, cameras, and DMC equipment
- Keep track of reservations for rooms and technology
- Keep track of fines and payments for overdue or lost items
- Provide a dashboard that shows current library activity: items on loan, overdue items, active reservations, and fines owed
